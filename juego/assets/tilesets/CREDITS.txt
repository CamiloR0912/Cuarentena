Map tilesets for Cuarentena - curated from free LPC packs and recolored to the Cuarentena-36 palette.
Maps (.tmx) are our own work, made with Tiled (https://www.mapeditor.org); Tiled's GPL covers the editor, not the maps.

- LPC Modern Streets - Faufilage - CC0 - https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-modern-streets
  (road, cracks, sidewalk, manhole, traffic markings, trash bins, traffic cones)
- LPC City Outside - Reemax, with Sharm (Lanea Zimmerman), Tuomo Untinen, Guido Bos, Johann C, Johannes Sjolund,
  Xenodora, William Thompson, daneeklu, wulax - CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 / GPL 2.0 - https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-city-outside
  (roofs, walls, gray windows, doors, towers, stone floor, lamp post, statue, obelisk, well; see its credits.txt)
- LPC Base Assets - Lanea "Sharm" Zimmerman, Stephen "Redshrike" Challener and others - CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/liberated-pixel-cup-lpc-base-assets-sprites-map-tiles (grass, trees; see CREDITS.TXT)
- LPC Modern Car Additions - Blue Moon Bear, based on "[LPC] Skorpio's SciFi Sprite Pack" by Skorpio and
  "LPC Police Car" by Baŝto - CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 - https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-modern-car-additions
- LPC Revised: The Office - Eliza Wyatt, with pieces by Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm) - CC-BY-SA 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-the-office (desk, laptop, copier, water cooler, TV, table, sink, bins)
- Futuristic Doctor's Office - spiral atlas, mostly edits of Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)'s LPC base assets - CC-BY-SA 4.0
  https://opengameart.org/node/96827 (hospital bed, IV stand, cabinets, bookshelf, counter, chair, plant, fridge, floors)
- Beds - bleutailfly & Jordan Irwin (AntumDeluge) - CC-BY-SA 3.0 or later - https://opengameart.org/content/beds
  (single beds used as stretchers)
- LPC Base Assets: inside.png (interior walls, door, wooden floor) - Lanea Zimmerman (Sharm)
