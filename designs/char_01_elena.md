## Visual description

- Woman, early 30s, medical resident after a 36-hour shift. She is a doctor, not a soldier: tired posture, determined.
- **White lab coat** (`N8` base, `N7`/`N6` shading) open over **clinical-blue scrubs** (`C1`, `C0` shadow). The coat is her signature: she must be the brightest figure on the dark asphalt.
- Dark brown hair (`W0`) in a low ponytail; mid skin tone (`S2`, `S1` shadow, `S3` highlight).
- Surgical mask pulled down around the neck (`C2`). Dark sneakers (`N1`).
- Small details readable at 1×: stethoscope (`N6`) around the neck, ID badge (`C1`) on the coat.
- Weapons are drawn **in her hands inside the attack/hold frames** (no separate weapon layer). In walk/idle she holds the currently equipped weapon only implicitly (empty hands) to keep frame count low.
